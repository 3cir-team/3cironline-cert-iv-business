# Certificate IV in Business: Is It Your Next Step?

A plain-English guide for people who coordinate work or guide others in a business role.

## What the qualification is

BSB40120 Certificate IV in Business is a nationally recognised AQF Level 4 qualification for people in business services roles who coordinate work or guide others. It has 12 units (6 core and 6 elective) and is studied online and self-paced, with practical workplace tasks instead of exams.

## Who it suits

It may suit you if you do admin or operational work and are being asked to coordinate others, if you already lead people informally and want a qualification to match, or if you want a Level 4 qualification built around your own workplace tasks and electives that suit your workplace.

## What to check before you enrol

Check who issues the qualification, compare the unit list on training.gov.au with the work you do, and confirm the entry requirement. For this course you need either a formal qualification or to complete a short language, literacy, numeracy and digital assessment.
